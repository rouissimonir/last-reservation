# TWO SIDES — The Missing Envelope

Player-visible case content, in unlock order. No solutions or ending.

## Introduction

Bellweather Hotel. A rainy evening in October 1994.

“A guest's sealed envelope has vanished from the archive. Three hotel employees had access tonight. One of you has the witness statements. The other has the hotel records. Work together to recover it before the guest returns.”

The supplied records are verified. Witness statements are accurate about what the witnesses directly observed. A clock reading is an observation, not necessarily the true time.

Choose a Witness Investigator (A) and a Records Investigator (B). Share one phone. Read privately, hide your evidence, then pass the phone. Describe your findings aloud at the shared question.

## Round 1 — When?

### A1 — Witness Investigator

A witness saw someone lift the identifiable sealed envelope from the archive shelf. The corridor clock read 22:14 at that exact moment. The witness could not identify the person. This is an observed clock reading, not necessarily true hotel time.

### B1 — Records Investigator

A maintenance note states that the corridor clock was exactly four minutes FAST throughout the evening. It was not adjusted until 23:00. Other official records use correct hotel time.

### Shared question

At what actual time was the envelope removed?

Options: 22:10 / 22:14 / 22:18.

## Round 2 — Who?

### A2 — Witness Investigator

A second witness clearly saw narrow striped cuffs on the same person who lifted the envelope from the archive shelf, now carrying it away. They did not see the person's face.

### B2 — Records Investigator

Verified access-and-uniform record, listing all three people who entered the archive tonight:

| Person | Cuffs | Verified presence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ada | Plain | 22:00–22:20 |
| Leo | Narrow striped | 22:12–22:30 |
| Nora | Narrow striped | 22:00–22:11 |

These are verified presence intervals, not planned shifts. No one else entered, and no uniform changes occurred.

### Shared question

Who removed the envelope?

Options: Ada / Leo / Nora.

## Round 3 — Where?

### A3 — Witness Investigator

A porter followed the carrier into the service passage. The carrier unlocked a storage compartment using a key marked B with a SQUARE head, placed the envelope inside, and locked it. The porter saw water dripping onto the archive shelving earlier, but does not know the compartment's name.

### B3 — Records Investigator

Verified storage-key register:

| Key | Head | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Square | Reception lost-property drawer |
| B | Round | Boiler-room tool cupboard |
| B | Square | Dry-document cabinet in the upstairs linen room |

The labels identify unique keys; there are no duplicate compartments.

### Shared question

Where should you look for the envelope?

Options: Reception lost-property drawer / Boiler-room tool cupboard / Upstairs linen-room dry-document cabinet.
