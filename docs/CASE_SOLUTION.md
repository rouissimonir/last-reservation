## Recovery and resolution

Only after the correct destination is chosen, the envelope is recovered dry and still sealed from that cabinet. A newly revealed signed note reads:

> A pipe was leaking over the archive shelf. I moved the envelope to the dry-document cabinet to keep it safe. I was called away before I could record the transfer. I'm sorry for the confusion.
>
> Nora · Bellweather Hotel

The note confirms motive after recovery. Earlier evidence establishes time, carrier and destination, not innocent intent. The three evidence chains are summarized after recovery. The guest's envelope can be returned; the case is fully resolved, with no purchase or cliffhanger.
