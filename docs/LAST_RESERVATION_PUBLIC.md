# The Last Reservation — player overview

Bellweather Hotel, 18 October 1994. Visitor Elin Ward promised two guests a meeting at ten. Her appointment now appears cancelled and she is nowhere in the lobby. The two guests must compare witness accounts and hotel records to find her.

One player reads the witness material; the other reads the records. Read privately, hide the sheet, then compare your findings aloud. There is no timer, sound requirement, penalty or purchase gate in this local development preview.

Six deductions, three acts:

1. **An appointment erased:** establish the visitor's appointment and follow her beyond reception.
2. **The altered ledger:** examine who had access and whether the cancellation can be trusted.
3. **A visitor found:** locate a retreat and decide what the surviving words actually establish.

Four relevant staff: Mara Vale, duty manager; Owen Bell, porter; Victor Hale, owner; Celia Rook, archivist. Reasons for suspicion are not proof of wrongdoing. Direct observations are accurate within their limits, certified records are reliable, and quoted assertions may require corroboration. The current appointment page is disputed. All necessary knowledge is inside the case.

The notebook records only confirmed deductions. Read sheets again through a neutral role handoff. Three progressive hints are available per deduction; the third explains the answer. Incorrect choices permit retry without advancing the case or revealing a hint automatically.

Full player wording is maintained in `src/game/last-reservation.ts`; author proofs, answers, timeline and ending are isolated in `LAST_RESERVATION_SOLUTION.md`. The app never imports these documents.

Target duration: 30–45 minutes, UNVALIDATED until two real players test it. This case has not yet had an independent narrative review or paired duration/fairness playtest.
