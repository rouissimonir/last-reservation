# Hint ratings and motion (19 October 2026)

## What a player sees

- **Per deduction:** up to three stars. 0 hints = 3 stars (Flawless), 1 hint = 2 (Sharp), 2 hints = 1 (Steady), the solution hint = 0 (Guided). The hint panel shows the live star count and says what each hint costs before it is taken; the confirmed screen shows the deduction's own result.
- **Per case:** a seal and title on the resolution screen, and a badge on the case card in the library.

| Tier | Rule | Seal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perfect Deduction | no hints at all | gold, three stars, halo, sheen, gold flecks, fanfare |
| Sharp Minds | score 80 or more | silver, two stars |
| Steady Hands | score 50 or more | bronze, one star |
| Case Solved | anything lower | slate, no stars |

Score = earned stars ÷ possible stars × 100. Wrong answers are never counted: the game still promises no penalty for trying again. Hints stay free; they only trade away stars. For The Missing Envelope (3 deductions) one hint scores 89 (Sharp), two hints in different deductions score 78 (Steady). `tests/rating.test.ts` proves that none, one and two hints rate differently on all eight cases.

## How it is built

- `src/game/rating.ts` is pure and derived from `Progress.hints`, which was already saved per deduction. **No save-format change, no migration**: existing saves are rated automatically, and `tests/rating.test.ts` pins the saved keys. Replaying a case resets its hints, so its rating is earned again.
- `src/components/motion.tsx` holds the animation primitives (`Entrance`, `PopIn`, `Stamp`, `Shake`, `Pulse`, `Shine`, `Sparkles`); `src/components/rating.tsx` the stars, per-deduction result and case seal; `src/components/launch-sequence.tsx` the game-start title card.
- All motion is decorative. With the OS reduced-motion preference on, every primitive renders its final state at once and the launch card is skipped. Every rating is also written as text (title, "2 of 3 stars", "1 hint used"); the star glyphs are hidden from screen readers.
- New sound cues `star` and `perfect` are generated by `scripts/generate-audio.ts`; the existing WAVs regenerate byte-identically.

## Where motion was added

Game start (title card: the keyhole emblem's ivory leaf swings open, drifts and settles slowly back as TWO and SIDES slide in, the seam flares, brass rule draws, tagline), library (staggered cards), case cover (staged build-up), roles, option selection (pulse), wrong answer (shake and gentle feedback), hint reveal (card rises in, a star dims), deduction confirmed (CONFIRMED stamp, stars pop in turn with chimes), resolution (seal stamps down, stars land, fanfare and sparkles for a perfect run). The private handoff scene is unchanged: it still shows instantly with no entrance.

## Not verified

No person has judged the new animations, chimes or fanfare by ear or on a device; timing and sound were checked only through Jest and a desktop-Chromium web build (frames captured at the stamp, sparkle and settled states, no console errors). Native haptics for the two new cues, reduced-motion behaviour on a real device, and animation performance on low-end phones are unverified. Replaying a case resets its live rating, but since 20 October the best rating and solve count are kept per case under `two-sides/<case id>/record/v1` (`src/game/records.ts`).
