## App Review notes (1277/4000 characters)

TWO SIDES is a cooperative mystery game for two people sharing one iPhone. No game account or reviewer login is required. Five cases are permanently free. The optional After Midnight Collection is one non-consumable purchase unlocking exactly three additional cases; no subscription is offered.

Free content: launch to the case library, select The Last Reservation, start the case and use each role's readiness button to read the two private sheets. Pass the phone between roles and discuss clues before answering. Incorrect answers allow retry without a penalty. The Notebook records confirmed deductions only. Evidence revisits and resumes use a neutral handoff.

Paid content: open After Midnight Collection from the library, then use Unlock all 3 with the localized store price. Restore Purchases is on that collection screen. The purchase is processed through StoreKit and RevenueCat. The three paid cases are The Room That Was Never Booked, The Last Call at Midnight and The Portrait Behind the Wall. Free cases do not require ownership. Resetting a case does not reset ownership.

Help & Privacy and Settings are reachable from the library. Case progress is stored on the device. Sound is optional. There is no user-generated content, chat or game-account registration.
