## Description

An envelope disappears. A piano falls silent. A room seems to have no guest.

At the Bellweather Hotel, every mystery has two sides—and neither investigator has the whole story.

TWO SIDES is a cooperative mystery game for two people sharing one phone. One of you reads witness accounts. The other studies hotel records. Keep your evidence private, describe what you learned, and decide together what really happened.

FIVE COMPLETE MYSTERIES, FREE

Begin with The Missing Envelope, then investigate The Last Reservation, The Silent Piano, The Masquerade Coat and The Music Box. Each case tells its own contained story.

AFTER MIDNIGHT

One optional, one-time in-app purchase adds three further mysteries: The Room That Was Never Booked, The Last Call at Midnight and The Portrait Behind the Wall. The After Midnight Collection includes these three cases and their hints. It is not a subscription and does not include future releases.

MADE FOR CONVERSATION

• Two players, one phone—no second device or game account needed.
• Separate private sheets and clear handoffs.
• Confirmed deductions collected in your Notebook.
• Three optional hints for every deduction, included.
• Local progress saves and replay for each case.
• Larger body text, no timer, and no sound required.

Take your time. Compare your stories. The answer lies between you.
