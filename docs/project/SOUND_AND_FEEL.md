# Sound and feel checkpoint (17 October 2026)

The owner asked to take the game "to a high level", possibly with music. Decision taken: a quiet, original soundtrack plus soft effects and haptics, all optional and on by default at low volume. No case content, case id, save key or save format changed.

## What was added

- **Soundtrack.** "Bellweather Nocturne", a 60-second seamless D-minor loop (electric piano, pad, bass, brushes, rain). Fades in after preferences load, fades out when switched off or when the app leaves the foreground, and never plays in the background.
- **Effects.** Eight short cues: tap (buttons), select (answer choice, settings), page (opening a private sheet or the next chapter), handoff (hiding evidence), confirm (correct deduction), wrong (gentle double knock), hint, solved (opening the resolution). Each cue reveals only what the screen already shows.
- **Haptics.** Soft impact on taps, selection ticks, success on a confirmed deduction or resolution, warning on a wrong answer. Phones only; hidden on web.
- **Settings.** New route `/settings` ("Sound & settings" on the library) and the same controls inside each case's Settings: music on/off with 5 volume levels (default 2), effects on/off with 5 levels (default 3), haptics on/off. Stored under `two-sides/preferences/v1`, separate from every case save; validated, written in order, and an unreadable record is kept untouched, with music off, until the player makes a new choice.
- **Silent mode.** Audio session uses `playsInSilentMode: false` and `mixWithOthers`, so the phone's silent setting is followed and other audio is not interrupted.
- **Feel.** Scene reveals now ease out over 280 ms (still skipped under reduced motion and never on the handoff screen). Help and settings copy updated: sound is optional and never needed to play.

## Sources and licence

All audio is generated by `scripts/generate-audio.ts`; nothing was downloaded or bought. Details and the regeneration check: `assets/audio/README.md`.

## Native configuration

`expo-audio` (~57.0.5) and `expo-haptics` (~57.0.3) were installed with `npx expo install`. The `expo-audio` plugin is configured with `microphonePermission: false`, `recordAudioAndroid: false` and `enableBackgroundPlayback: false`; `npx expo config --type introspect` shows no microphone string, no RECORD_AUDIO, no UIBackgroundModes and no foreground service. Remaining permissions: MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS (expo-audio) and VIBRATE (expo-haptics), both normal install-time permissions.

## Not verified

- No sound was listened to by a person. The generator's output was checked numerically (length, peak level, loop seam, deterministic regeneration), not by ear.
- No native build was made. Silent-switch behaviour, haptics, fades, the loop seam on device players and the App Store's handling of expo-audio's recorder code (possible microphone purpose-string warning) must be checked on a signed iPhone build and an Android device.
- Audio is 2.9 MB of WAV because no encoder is available here; an AAC conversion would make it much smaller.
