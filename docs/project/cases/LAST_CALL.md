## Characters and knowledge

Ruth (sounds and call timing), Penrose (sightline across the roof; weather), Vera Lund (one hooded person used the roof door; cannot say who), Mara (key safe; Teddy's flat torch), Carl (honest round; did not know Teddy was inside), Teddy (inside, unhurt).

## Evidence and deductions

1. **LC1 — The roof plant room.** A: dripping into a tin tray, two start-stop rumbles, no bell, call running past the second rumble. B: group C's four phones and their sounds; lift log. Cellar has no machinery; boiler is dry and rumbles constantly; laundry's bell rang during the call.
2. **LC2 — Wedge removed, door latched itself** (LC1). A: Penrose saw a figure bend at the door before it swung shut; still air; could not exclude a key. B: no keyhole; closer latches when unwedged (or if a gust swings it); inside lever removed.
3. **LC3 — Carl Brandt** (LC2; reuses LC-A2). A: one hooded person unlocked the roof door from the stairs and came back minutes later; nobody else used it. B: roof-door counter 23:41/23:53/23:56; key 1 with Teddy (on the roof); key 2 handed to Carl in person at 23:52; Mara at the front desk all the time.
4. **LC4 — Behind the water tank, at the shelves** (LC3). A: Carl's threshold sweep saw nobody; Teddy's flat torch. B: what a torch reaches from the threshold; shelves hidden behind the tank; case kept there; inside light switched from outside, off.
5. **LC5 — Carl switched off the roof circuit** (LC4). A: call 00:00:05 + 35 s, faded not clicked, board kept working. B: circuit off at 00:00:40, feeds the plant-room line; wire intact.

## Ending

Teddy found behind the tank with the case. Carl's round-book entry: he could not see Teddy, switched off the circuit not knowing, takes the blame, sets new rules. Confirms what Carl knew; no one meant harm.
