## Ground truth and timeline (1 November 1994)

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 21:00 | Kitchen porter Ruth Adeyemi weighs tureen (5 kg), tray (6 kg) and wrapped music box together (14.5 kg → box 3.5 kg). The tureen slops soup over the wrapping. She sends the dumbwaiter up. |
| 21:10 | Waiter Remy Gault rings for the dumbwaiter at the second-floor pantry. |
| 21:12 | Chambermaid Lucy Marsh, passing the first-floor hatch, sees soup soaking the paper, opens the hatch and takes the box out (car 14.5 → 11.0 kg). |
| 21:12 | Pastry chef Bruno Sale is on the staff stairs with the cake; signs it in at 21:13. |
| 21:13–21:20 | Lucy carries the dripping box left round the corner to the pressing room (third door on the right) to dry and rewrap it. |
| 21:14 | Car arrives at the pantry with 11.0 kg (tureen and tray). |
| 21:20 | Mrs Achebe (Room 105, chained in since 21:00) opens her door and follows the drips. |
| 21:40 | Duty manager takes statements; Lucy says she was turning down Room 105. |

## Characters and knowledge

Ruth (load and weights; can't see the car after it leaves), Remy and Bruno (truthful about 21:12), Lucy (lies about her whereabouts because staff must not touch room service), Mrs Achebe (followed the trail; saw nobody).

## Evidence allocation

- MB1 · A: Ruth's weights (whole 14.5, tureen 5, tray 6). B: load drum (14.5 out of the kitchen, 11.0 after the first-floor hatch, 11.0 on arrival; no other hatch).
- MB2 · A: three statements about 21:12. B: Room 105 turndown at 19:30 and chained door 21:00–21:20; cake signed in at 21:13; pantry bell 21:10/21:14. Records do not say who claimed what.
- MB3 · A: Mrs Achebe's directions (from the hatch, left at the corner, third door on the right). B: first-floor plan listing the right-hand doors in order.

## Deductions

1. **MB1 — At the first-floor hatch.** 14.5 − 5 − 6 = 3.5 kg; the car lost exactly 3.5 kg there and arrived with 11 kg. Kitchen ruled out (14.5 left); pantry ruled out (only 11 kg arrived). A alone cannot place the drop; B alone cannot identify a 3.5 kg item.
2. **MB2 — Lucy Marsh.** Room 105 was already turned down and its guest admitted nobody. Bruno's and Remy's accounts match their records.
3. **MB3 — The pressing room.** Third right-hand door after the left turn (linen room, Room 109, pressing room).

## Ending

Pressing room: the paper drying on the rail, the box unharmed, fresh paper and Lucy's note (soup soaking it; afraid of the room-service rule). Motive only in the note.
