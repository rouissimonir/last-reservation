# The Portrait Behind the Wall — author-only specification

Edition 08 · id `the-portrait-behind-the-wall` · version 1 · save key `two-sides/the-portrait-behind-the-wall/v1` · **After Midnight Collection** (paid). Contains answers. Playable text and hints: `src/game/portrait-behind-wall.ts`.

Primary mechanic: **document provenance and sequence** — watermark year marks, letterhead editions, a newspaper lining, typewriter faults, registers, actual boarding and a verified marriage certificate. Provisional design target 40–60 minutes (unvalidated; not advertised).

Canon: founder Ambrose Bellweather and his daughter Clara are new to the series and do not conflict with earlier cases. Celia Rook (archivist) is the same character as in The Last Reservation; nothing here spoils that case.

## Characters and knowledge

Tobias Wren (restorer: describes, never dates), Celia Rook (archive records), June Reed (family memory — a claim until supported), Ambrose (forger; note confirms his knowledge and feelings), Clara and Jonah (historical), Edith Marlow (secretary, typewriter No. 1).

## Evidence and deductions

1. **PB1 — Letter D.** A: each sheet's date, letterhead and watermark mark. B: year-mark key (non-alphabetical symbols), letterhead editions (old one used up March 1936), paper never used before its year. Letter C's older stock is the deliberate decoy.
2. **PB2 — Clara Bellweather.** A: portrait (round window, "C. … J. R., 1934"), Letters A and B ("your father's own writing room", "this house"). B: the three C's at the hotel in 1934 and their fathers; the writing room was Ambrose's study; the round window is unique to it.
3. **PB3 — In March 1936, before the job was signed off** (PB2). A: panel never removed; Gazette of 2 March 1936 pasted behind it; JOB 211 batten. B: job 211 ran 10 February–20 March 1936; no other work there.
4. **PB4 — Ambrose Bellweather** (PB1, PB3; reuses PB-A1). A: Letter D's broken "e" and dropped "T"; Letter C clean. B: No. 1 clean; No. 2 has both faults and was bought October 1938. A verified use register assigns No. 2 exclusively to Ambrose through the sealed May 1939 deposit, which he presented as Clara’s 1933 original. Residence is not used as an access alibi.
5. **PB5 — She left on the 7:10 to marry Jonah Reed, against her father's wishes** (PB1, PB2, PB4; reuses PB-A2). A: C482’s transcribed names and marriage date, with June’s account; no journey time. B: actual 7:10 boarding on 7 September and independent verification of C482, without reproducing its names/date. A leaves three journeys; B leaves marriage versus the non-marriage alternative. Together one answer survives.

## Ending

The backing board of the frame holds Ambrose's March 1936 note: she married her painter, he disowned her, cannot burn the picture, "let the wall keep it… tell her I was wrong". It confirms he knew she had married, so the 1939 history was knowingly false. June requests a copy and a corrected caption naming Clara and Jonah together.
